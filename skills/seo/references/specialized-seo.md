# Specialized SEO: local, e-commerce, international

## Local SEO

Audit:

- accurate business name/address/phone/service area and consistent site contact data;
- location/service pages with genuinely distinct local value;
- Google Business Profile information when the user provides access/data or live evidence is available;
- local business structured data matching visible business facts;
- reviews/reputation patterns without manufacturing or gating reviews;
- local landing-page content, hours, directions, service boundaries, and conversions;
- duplicate location pages, fake offices, doorway city pages, or thin location sprawl.

Do not recommend fake addresses, virtual-office deception, or mass city pages with swapped place names.

## E-commerce SEO

Audit:

- product/category architecture and internal linking;
- indexation strategy for products, categories, filters/facets, search pages, out-of-stock/discontinued items;
- product titles/descriptions/specs and unique merchant value;
- product/offer/variant structured data accuracy;
- image quality/discoverability;
- variant URLs and canonicals;
- price, currency, availability, shipping/returns consistency where surfaced;
- pagination/infinite scroll crawlability;
- duplicate manufacturer copy at scale;
- Merchant Center/free-listing integration where applicable;
- discontinued-product redirect/removal strategy based on substitutes, demand, and permanence.

Do not canonicalize all variants/categories to a parent if distinct pages legitimately serve distinct user/search needs.

## International and multilingual SEO

Audit:

- stable, crawlable locale-specific URLs;
- correct language/region targeting;
- hreflang clusters with self-references and return links;
- valid language/region codes;
- `x-default` when appropriate;
- canonicals pointing to the correct same-language/locale canonical;
- localized content rather than template-only translation where user expectations differ;
- internal locale links and language switchers;
- geo/language redirects that do not prevent crawlers/users from accessing alternate versions;
- local currency, shipping, legal, and product differences where relevant.

Use one maintainable hreflang implementation method rather than duplicating HTML/header/sitemap implementations without reason.
