# Keyword, SERP, competitor, and content-gap research

## Principles

Keyword research is demand and intent research, not a license to create one page per phrase.

When live search/SERP tools are available, use current results. Record locale/device/date when materially relevant. Search results are personalized and dynamic; avoid overgeneralizing from one snapshot.

## Keyword workflow

1. Start with the product/service/topic and actual audience.
2. Collect query families from first-party Search Console data first when available.
3. Expand with live SERP suggestions, related questions/searches, competitor coverage, and reputable keyword datasets if accessible.
4. Group by underlying intent and topic, not just lexical similarity.
5. Map one primary page purpose to each durable intent cluster.
6. Identify whether the right action is: improve existing page, consolidate cannibalizing pages, create a new page, or do nothing.

## Intent classes

Use practical intent labels as aids, not rigid taxonomies:

- informational / learning;
- problem-solving / troubleshooting;
- comparison / evaluation;
- commercial investigation;
- transaction/action;
- navigational / brand;
- local / visit;
- post-purchase/support.

## Competitor analysis

Compare specific attributes rather than “copying top-ranking pages”:

- search intent satisfied;
- unique information or tools;
- content format and information architecture;
- freshness and factual sourcing;
- internal linking / category structure;
- page experience;
- structured data/search appearance;
- media and first-party assets;
- brand/entity trust signals.

Differentiate direct business competitors from SERP competitors such as forums, publishers, marketplaces, documentation, and video platforms.

## Content gaps

A gap is worthwhile when the user's site has a credible reason to satisfy the intent better. Reject ideas that are off-topic, duplicative, low-value, or would create scaled thin content.

## Cannibalization

Do not flag two pages merely because they share keywords. Look for substantially overlapping intent, content, and search performance. Remedies may include clearer differentiation, internal-link changes, consolidation/redirect, or canonicalization only when pages are genuinely duplicates.

## Deliverable

For keyword/content plans, include:

- cluster/intent;
- recommended existing/new URL;
- user goal;
- why the site is qualified to answer;
- differentiated value to add;
- supporting subtopics/entities;
- internal link sources/targets;
- measurement target.
